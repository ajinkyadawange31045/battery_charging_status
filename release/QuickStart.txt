🔋 BatteryGuard Pro — Portable Battery Charging & Health Monitor
========================================================================

Thank you for downloading BatteryGuard Pro!

QUICK START:
1. Double-click "BatteryGuard.exe" to launch.
2. Set your Low Alert (e.g. 15%) and High Alert (e.g. 90%) thresholds.
3. Choose your alarm sound (Digital Alarm, Melodic Chime, Siren, or Custom WAV).
4. Click "Save Settings".
5. Click "Hide to Tray" (or click 'X') — it runs silently in your Windows system tray!

ZERO INSTALLATION & PORTABLE:
- This is a single standalone file (~69 KB).
- You can copy "BatteryGuard.exe" to a USB drive or other Windows laptops.
- Works natively on Windows 11 and Windows 10 with zero dependencies.

RESOURCE USAGE:
- CPU: 0.0% idle (pure event-driven)
- RAM: ~18-25 MB (drops to ~8-15 MB in system tray)
- Battery Impact: < 0.001% (uses virtually no battery power)

FEATURES INCLUDED:
- ⚡ High Battery Alarm (90% default) — auto-stops when charger is unplugged.
- 🪫 Low Battery Alarm (15% default) — auto-stops when charger is plugged in.
- 📊 1-Click Windows Official Battery Health & Cycle Life Report.
- 🌙 Quiet Hours (Mutes audio alerts overnight so you can sleep undisturbed).
- ⚡ 100% Full Charge Reminder (Prevents continuous high-voltage saturation).
- ⏰ Smart Snooze (5m/10m) & Stop Alarm controls.
- 🚀 Start with Windows automatically.
- 🎨 Modern Windows 11 Fluent Light UI with dynamic battery gauge.
